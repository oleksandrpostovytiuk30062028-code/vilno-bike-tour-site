FAQ

Questions, answered

A few things teams often want to know before they begin.

How do I get started with the free plan?

Click Get Started and follow the signup steps. No credit card is required for the free plan.

Can I upgrade my plan at any time?

Yes. You can change your plan from your account dashboard whenever your needs change.

What payment methods do you accept?

Paid plans support major credit cards and PayPal through the billing portal.

Is my data safe with your service?

We use modern security practices and access controls to help protect team information.

Do you offer a refund policy?

Paid plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee. Contact support if the service is not right for you.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel from account settings; access continues through the current billing period.
